InfoAware e-learning

What was my role?

Creating e-learning experiences and refreshing the digital brand.

I worked as an e-learning and graphic designer, creating interactive training courses, bespoke illustrations and iconography while improving the visual identity of the website and learning materials.

How did I achieve this?

Designed interactive learning experiences.

Used Adobe Captivate and Articulate Storyline to create accessible courses that made complex information easier to understand.

Developed illustrations and iconography.

Produced bespoke visual assets using Adobe Illustrator and InDesign, making educational content clearer and more visually engaging.

Refreshed the digital experience.

Improved the website’s visual appearance with a consistent brand identity across marketing and learning materials.

How things improved?

Clearer and more accessible learning experiences.

Structured educational content into manageable sections with intuitive interactions, making courses easier to navigate and understand.

Stronger visual identity.

Introduced a more modern, cohesive visual language through custom illustrations and iconography.

Compelling educational content.

Combined instructional design with visual storytelling to make digital training more approachable, practical and captivating.
