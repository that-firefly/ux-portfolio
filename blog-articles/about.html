About Estela DrawWilder

Draw Wilder Lines is a designer original brand by Estela Gaspar, a natural born artist

Born in the historic, picturesque city of Leiria, Portugal famed for its stunning medieval castle perched high above the landscape, I bring the vibrant energy and cultural heritage of my roots and life story to my work as a dual British citizen.

I began sketching and painting instinctively as a child, completely self-taught. For me, Art became a vital language of resilience, a way to process and rise above the silent echoes of childhood sexual abuse and domestic violence.

I paint completely freehand without relying on preliminary sketches. By applying my art directly onto upcycled leather bags and lampshades, I transform ordinary items into stunning, unique statement pieces for your wardrobe or home. These are creations you truly won't find anywhere else on the planet, uncannily personal art that a machine simply cannot replicate because it is driven entirely by human emotion.

My Art captures is human (no AI or algorithms involved) and expresses raw emotion and energy (not code), which comes alive through the powerful forms of my flowers and the fluid motion of my background waves.

My signature aesthetic relies on capturing the organic flow, wavy patterns, and living currents of the human emotions and the natural world rather than static realism, transforming that untamed energy into magnificent, one-of-a-kind statement pieces whose unique c creative vision bridges the raw majesty of nature with bespoke interior design.

When sketching and painting portraits of people and animals, I strive to capture the immediate state of their soul, embedding that raw essence directly into the artwork.

I regularly showcase and sell my work at select venues and art markets across Cambridge and London.

From flowing, wearable luxury scarves and bespoke fabric designs to elegant decorative pillows, my colourful and striking artwork infuses soul and character into spaces. Whether tailoring sophisticated accents for private homes, creating an inviting ambiance for cosy coffee shops and restaurants, or elevating the visual identity of luxury hotels, mycreations seamlessly blend >fine Art with functional Design.

My vision extends across a comprehensive range of branded environments and corporate spaces. Through immersive indoor and outdoor signage, architectural window graphics, and custom vehicle wraps, to transform everyday touchpoints into moving masterpieces. From high impact roller-banners and dynamic pop-up displays for exhibitions to bespoke office decorations and art installations, every application is meticulously crafted to tell a powerful visual story.
