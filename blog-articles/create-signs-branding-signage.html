Comprehensive new branding for Create Signs Ltd, a premier signage and vehicle graphics company based in Cambridge.

The goal was to build a powerful visual identity for Create Signs Ltd that matches their 30-year legacy of industry expertise across their Waterbeach and Bar Hill branches.

The core concept behind the rebranding was to bridge the Create Signs name with the literal creation of the universe and its cosmic signs. By drawing a parallel between the birth of elements in the cosmos and the crafting of physical signage, the identity positions the brand as an elemental, originating force in design.

This refreshed identity was strategically developed to scale seamlessly across all of their core service offerings, from high-impact interior and exterior shop signs to vibrant commercial vehicle wraps and embroidered workwear.

The final design delivers a sharp, modern aesthetic that captures the company's creative precision and positions Create Signs Ltd as the go to partner for businesses looking to make an unforgettable first impression.
