Cottage Flowers

Drawwilder Drawwilder Pinterest

Cottage Flowers was created from my original artwork, inspired from garden flowers drawn usually in the morning or at dusk.

I always start my products from an original pen sketch. No AI or artificial sources.

I usually scan the original design or work it further with either art materials or digital software but without altering the initial artwork too much.

The original lines are always the most honest ones and they are really hard to recreate. They are the embodiment of what makes this lampshade so unique.

The Cottage Flowers lampshade was commissioned by a customer that loves the way the lampshade (linen-look fabric) disperses the light into the room, transforming the space into a mysterious but also cosy and private environment.

Each lampshade is thoughtfully designed and handcrafted using my original artwork

I offer a range of sizes, from smaller round shades (20cmØ x 18.5cm) to statement pieces (40cmØ x 25cm), as well as elegant oval designs (30 x 22 x 20cm), suitable for both ceiling and table lamps, including USA harp finial fittings.

You can choose from my existing designs or collaborate with me to create something entirely personal, with guidance available if you would like help deciding.

All pieces are printed on high-quality fabrics, including a beautiful linen-look rustic fabric and luxurious velvet.
