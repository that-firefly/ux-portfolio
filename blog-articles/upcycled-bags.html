Upcycled hand-drawn bags by Estela Gaspar

I paint completely freehand without relying on preliminary sketches. By applying my art directly onto upcycled leather bags and lampshades, I transform ordinary items into stunning, unique statement pieces for your wardrobe or home. These are creations you truly won't find anywhere else on the planet, uncannily personal art that a machine simply cannot replicate because it is driven entirely by human emotion.

I personally select high quality, pre-loved bags for my canvas. After a thorough cleaning, I hand-paint each piece freehand using acrylic pens, then seal the artwork with a protective coating to ensure it lasts.
