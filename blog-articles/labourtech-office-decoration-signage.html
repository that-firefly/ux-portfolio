Transforming spaces: a complete office & brand refresh for Labour-tech Recruitment Ltd

Award-winning recruitment specialists Labour-tech Recruitment transformed their Ely office branch with a fresh, vibrant, and highly professional new look. The comprehensive branding refresh seamlessly tied together their physical office space with their dynamic brand identity, using their signature green and purple color palette.

The project scope delivered high impact brand touchpoints across every corner of their space, including:

  • Striking Outdoor Signage.Vibrant external signage alongside engaging window graphics.
  • Sleek indoor signage.A premium acrylic wall plaque for the interior walls and clean, dimensional reception lettering that makes a brilliant first impression the moment clients and candidates step through the door.
  • Portable exhibition banners.Dynamic, high-quality roller banners designed to clearly highlight their services.
  • Branded promotional counters.Custom wrapped reception booth and podium that provides a clean, functional, and highly professional workstation for greeting visitors.
