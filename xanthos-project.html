Xanthos Digital Marketing Agency

What was my role?

Leading creative direction, delivering e-commerce and user centred design.

As a Senior Designer, I operated at creative lead and creative director level , managing a small design team, leading client meetings and translating business objectives into creative solutions across B2B and B2C sectors.

My work covered UX/UI design, e-commerce, branding, digital marketing and book cover design.

How did I achieve this?

Translated business goals into creative solutions.

Led client meetings to understand business needs, define project requirements and establish a clear creative direction.

Designed and delivered user-friendly digital experiences.

Created wireframes, prototypes and responsive websites using Axure, WordPress and Kentico, balancing usability with commercial objectives.

Led creative projects from concept to delivery.

Managed designers across multiple projects, developing visual identities, marketing materials and book covers for best-selling IT publications.

How things improved?

Clearer brand communication.

Helped clients present their products, services and ideas through engaging, consistent visual experiences.

Greater creative quality and consistency.

Combined creative direction, UX strategy and hands-on design to maintain high standards across digital and print projects.

Contributed to clients’ business growth objectives.

Delivered accessible websites, e-commerce experiences and marketing materials designed to strengthen clients’ online presence and commercial performance.
