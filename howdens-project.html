Howdens Joinery

What was my role?

Improving engagement, accessibility, consistency and digital experiences.

I worked across UX, UI and graphic design , supporting the evolution of the Howdens website, design system and digital marketing materials to meet user needs and business objectives.

How did I achieve this?

Maintained and evolved a complex design system.

Used Abstract for version control and collaborative design, ensuring consistency across websites, apps and trader applications.

Improved collaboration between designers, developers and ensuring a smoother design handover.

Used Sketch, Figma, Anima and Zeplin to create and refine interfaces, supporting accurate and efficient implementation.

Collaborated within a multidisciplinary team.

Worked with UX researchers, product designers and marketing teams, using research insights to improve sales through accessible, functional, visually cohesive digital experiences.

How things improved?

Greater accessibility and design consistency.

Contributed to more accessible and cohesive digital experiences through reusable components and shared design standards.

Improved design collaboration and delivery.

Version control and a clearer design handover improved collaboration across teams, enabling continuous platform improvements.

Contributed to business growth.

Website and marketing enhancements focused on attracting more visitors, improving customer engagement and increasing conversion rates.
