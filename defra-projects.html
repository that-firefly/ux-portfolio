DEFRA

What was my role?

To understand both business and user needs, shaping journeys, improving clarity, and translating complex regulatory processes into more structured and usable service experiences on Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR), Packaging Recovery Notes (PRN), and Materials Facilities.

How did I achieve this?

Built a strong understanding of the regulatory context, user needs, and the specific problems the service needed to solve. This included reviewing previous research, documentation, user interviews, and workshop outputs, as well as collaborating closely with stakeholders across the project.

Created user flow diagrams, empathy maps, task maps, discovery journey maps, and page flows aligned with the UK Government Digital Service (GDS) Design System. I also updated online prototypes using the GOV.UK Prototype Kit, GitHub, and Nunjucks, helping connect research, flows, and interface design into functional prototype journeys.

A core part of the process involved identifying the real users of the service, particularly producers and regulators, and understanding their objectives, behaviours, and pain points across platforms such as GOV.UK services and Power BI environments.

I also created user task diagrams and data maps to visualise the relationship between user goals, data flow, and service interactions, helping bring more alignment and clarity to the wider system.

How things improved?

Made complex environmental compliance services more understandable.

Helped uncover user needs and translate them into clearer journeys, flows, and prototype experiences.
